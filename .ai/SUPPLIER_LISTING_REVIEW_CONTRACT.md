# Supplier listing drift: bounded Design Contract (2026-11-06)

## Scope and authority

Implement only a read-only bound `supplierListingReview` projection from a frozen evidence manifest, then show the same assessment in Evidence, picker, correspondence, completion and purchasing. Do not persist this annotation in Part metadata or invoke `save_part_successor` for listing drift. This is one axis of `.ai/ALL_PARTS_REVIEW_CONTRACT.md`, covering all 19,611 active Parts against captured official listings. It does not establish full review, visual verification, physical calibration, replacement identity, current stock or purchasing readiness. No registry regeneration, sale-policy changes, re-registration, image processing, or automatic adoption of supplier values.

The current-supplier report is provisional until direct-request receipts have been reclassified: pagination absence alone does not establish disappearance; HTTP429 is retryable transport uncertainty, never unavailable. Use final per-entry receipts, not the present `unavailableProducts` aggregate. Freeze the final review report/receipts and baseline hashes before planning writes. Capture timestamps describe a window, not a simultaneous live snapshot. No wall-clock TTL or claim of perpetual freshness is introduced.

## Evidence and projected assessment

Use `version:1`, `recipe:'supplier-listing-drift-v1'`, and `status:'needs-review'` with a nonempty sorted set of codes for flagged entries. Exhaustively compared unchanged entries use `status:'compared-unchanged'`, empty codes, and the same evidence bindings; that status covers only captured compared fields. Supported codes are `SKU_CHANGED`, `PRICE_CHANGED`, `PURCHASE_LABEL_CHANGED`, `SIZE_LABEL_CHANGED`, `REFERENCE_IMAGE_NO_LONGER_IN_PRODUCT_GALLERY`, `PRODUCT_BODY_HTML_CHANGED`, `CURRENT_PRODUCT_ENDPOINT_404`, `CURRENT_VARIANT_NOT_LISTED`, and `CURRENT_FETCH_UNRESOLVED`. Never accept the report's generic `CURRENT_PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE` without checking its receipt. Endpoint404 means only the captured endpoint returned404; it is not proof of discontinuation. Variant-not-listed requires a successful parsed200 response for the exact product and an exhaustive missing variant ID. Same product/variant IDs do not prove unchanged commercial identity when SKU or packaging changes; IDs are evidence keys, not replacement-equivalence rules.

Manifest header: workspaceId, registrySha256, baselinePartSetSha256, version and evidence-manifest hash. Entry fields: sourceDraftId, productId, variantId, sourceUrl, registrySha256, sourceObjectSha256; baselinePartId and baselineBusinessSha256; reviewReportSha256, reviewEntrySha256; receipt `{url,finalUrl,fetchedAt,status,sha256}`; codes; reviewedAt; reviewer/producer version. Include only actual known receipt fields. Keep optional observed SKU/price/size/purchase text as evidence, never merge them into Part or specs. HTTP200 data must parse and bind to the expected product ID; redirects to another product or malformed bodies are unresolved, not matching evidence. Product-body byte changes remain source-only drift until separately assessed semantically; do not infer a hole, dimension, fit, purchase condition or formatting-only equivalence. Freeze the explicit judgments for the 49 visible-text change groups in body-semantic-groups.json: each changed Part must carry `bodySemanticReview:{status,groups,effects,reasons,evidenceHashes,reviewedAt,reviewer}`. Status is unreviewed/reviewed; effects are formatting-only, descriptive, composition-consumption, physical-form-fit or unresolved. A text group may have multiple effects. Any composition/quantity/consumption change blocks purchase estimates. Physical-form-fit changes block purchase readiness pending identity/fit review without altering physical facts. Unreviewed/unresolved body changes are blocked until a documented judgment establishes no effect on purchasing. Examples such as set composition, supplied connectors and size/holes cannot be dismissed as formatting. These judgments do not certify old or new physical definitions.

Define baselineBusinessSha256 with canonical sorted JSON over the Part's business fields and purchase/path/connection/hole contents; omit only server identity/ownership/timestamps/archive/supersedes authority fields, `supplierListingReview`, and generated child row identity/parent keys. Preserve all other metadata, array order and business values. Define and test this normalization once. Assets bind by existing asset IDs and their immutable original/high/medium/thumbnail bytes. Historical source snapshots remain preserved on their existing rows; the RPC does not automatically clone them to a successor. Evidence references immutable registry/source/report hashes rather than falsely claiming new source-snapshot adoption.

The projection loader verifies workspace ownership, registry/source/report/entry/receipt hashes and source identity, exactly one intended Part ID, and current business fingerprint before returning an assessment. Freeze the baseline row hash as evidence too; authority/timestamp-only differences are not a new business definition. No matching by product ID, SKU, URL or sourceDraftId alone across differing definitions. Any mismatch is `SUPPLIER_LISTING_REVIEW_STALE`; return an unresolved assessment for affected official-source Parts, capture the reason and continue independent valid entries. Domain writes are always zero. HTTP429/timeout/403/5xx remain `CURRENT_FETCH_UNRESOLVED` after bounded evidence capture/retry; no unavailable count. Do not fabricate empty evidence or publish unchecked200 as unchanged.

## Assessment and freshness behavior

A single pure domain helper returns notice, observed codes, evidence timestamp, binding status and purchasing gate. Server projection validates private hashes before handing bounded facts to the client. Validity requires supported schema/recipe, sourceDraftId/product/variant/source URL binding, matching business fingerprint and valid evidence references. A copied assessment on a different Part must fail. Browser components must not claim file-hash verification they cannot perform.

Evidence validity is content-bound, not time-based. Display the captured date and historical-reference status. Later known source/definition/evidence changes invalidate the assessment; they never silently mark it reviewed. Invalid/stale entries retain a visible '掲載情報の照合が失効しています' review warning and suppress purchasing as unresolved; they do not clear a hold or restore a price. A missing assessment for a reviewed official source is review-unavailable; it never means all review passed. Manual Parts outside the supplier review preserve prior behavior. A newer verified receipt may replace an unresolved transport classification only in a newly frozen review entry; no status is silently promoted to supported/fullReviewComplete. Reverting to unchanged supplier values also needs a new bound assessment and separate explicit reconciliation; this operation only records needs-review.

| Evidence category | Current purchasing gate | Display |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SKU, price, purchase-label or size-label change; product endpoint404; missing exact variant | blocked | Current purchase terms/identity require confirmation |
| Unresolved fetch or stale/invalid known assessment | blocked | Current listing could not be confirmed; transport errors are not unavailability |
| Body drift with composition-consumption/physical-form-fit effect, or body meaning unreviewed/unresolved | blocked | Composition, fit or purchase meaning requires confirmation |
| Gallery-only drift, or body drift explicitly judged formatting-only/descriptive with no economic/fit effect; exact SKU/price/size/purchase fields unchanged in valid200 receipt | existing historical calculation allowed | Published evidence changed; image/other review remains pending |

All purchase calculations are reference estimates from retained registration terms. No unchanged comparison establishes live stock or guaranteed purchase price. For blocked items return `purchaseUnits:null`, `purchasePrice:null`, and an explicit review reason, preserving node counts and path lengths. Exclude them from known-price totals and increment unknown counts. Do not show0 as a substitute. Retain existing physical/weight behavior; drift does not validate or overwrite geometry, image scale, hole/connection or weight facts. Existing schematic-wire suppression and prior holds remain at least as restrictive.

Historical registered prices, SKU and purchase specs remain inspectable in Evidence as '登録時の情報（現在の購入計算には使用しません）'. Do not render an automatically calculated historical bag count or historical total in the purchase/BOM flow for blocked items: it would look actionable. A future explicit historical-analysis feature is outside scope. Gallery-only or explicitly non-economic/non-fit body judgments can keep the existing calculation with the captured-date warning. Body byte drift alone neither proves a semantic change nor clears possible economic/fit effects.

## Read-only persistence boundary and referenced designs

Choose one approach: a frozen evidence manifest plus read-only projection; no Part metadata writes or successors. This is a status annotation, not adoption of a new supplier definition. It preserves all existing native Part/spec/hole/connection IDs and source snapshots, avoids successor/child-ID churn, and avoids the archived-predecessor coverage hole. Keep `supplierListingReview` as an optional top-level domain property mapped from response `supplier_listing_review`, analogous to selection_reference in `projectLocalAggregate` and `partRepository`; never serialize it into native Part save payloads. Existing ordinary saves preserve their current semantics.

The manifest covers all baseline19,611 Part IDs with per-entry assessment outcome, including unchanged compared terms; only flagged entries show drift warnings. Unchanged means compared captured fields only, not full review. Keep a finite flagged index and lookup by workspace+PartID+source+business hash, not 'first active Part for this source'. Include archived Parts used by Designs when they match an explicitly indexed baseline entry. Root evidence currently identifies one affected referenced Part, 9360fd0a-248a-471e-9695-9369e289c0fc; its PURCHASE_LABEL_CHANGED gate must be delivered directly to its existing ID. All Design IDs/revisions/part references and all DB rows remain byte-for-byte unchanged by this operation. Do not fetch successor data or relink old Designs.

Cover aggregate list/get, replayed reads, direct Part select used by catalog/correspondence, archived-used Part loading, and repository mapping. For partial column queries, internally fetch the full binding fields needed for verification while returning only authorized projection fields. `partCorrespondence` receives the same status without changing promotion IDs, registry, sale groups or historical mappings. `selectionReference` can expose a canonical offer different from retainedSourceDraftId: assess that exact source object and frozen offer terms too before showing its purchase price. Uncertainty is blocked, never bypassed by the historical minimum-sale policy. Sale policy remains unchanged.

The sidecar is required review evidence for this workspace. Store its bytes and referenced receipts under a declared workspace path; package them with portable backup as explicit checksummed review dependencies and validate them on restore. Do not embed runtime absolute paths as authority. Missing, corrupt, wrong-workspace, changed-registry or unsupported-version sidecars yield `review-unavailable` for official-source Parts and quotes in this reviewed workspace, with warning and suppressed purchasing; Part/Design display/edit/save remains available. Do not report missing sidecar as clean or reviewed. A fresh workspace with no official sources remains unaffected. A preexisting official workspace that has not been reviewed reports review-unavailable rather than pretending this review ran. Database-only restore may work but cannot claim preserved listing-review coverage. Restoring valid evidence restores assessment without any DB mutation. Backup acceptance must prove sidecar inclusion/restoration, or explicitly leave portable review readiness incomplete.

The shared manifest/source-offer index serves cord quote assessments with exact source/object hashes and observed terms. It is not a generic sourceDraftId equivalence map and may not apply one Part's drift to a different definition. Same source with changed business fingerprint is unresolved. Ordinary user edits never auto-rebase a review entry. A new adoption/change contract and normal CAS successor flow are required only if future work changes business definitions; that is outside this contract. The inspected SQL successor clones some child values with new IDs and archives referenced predecessors; using it for this annotation would add risk with no business benefit.
